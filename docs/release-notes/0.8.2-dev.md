# Matchframe Esports 0.8.2-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Fixed from your 0.8.1 recording (two rounds on Ancient); not yet seen in a real CS2.**

**Coming from 0.8.1:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded, then **restart CS2** (it reads the new game-data settings when it starts).

## Cinematics move now

**Why they stood still (fixed):** HLAE tells its scripts how long each frame took, about 0.005 s at your frame rate. Matchframe's HLAE script read that number as a clock. So "time into the shot" stayed at zero, and HLAE drew every shot at its first frame. That covered every HLAE shot since 0.7.1: the team glides, the pause glides and all the demo camera paths. In your recording, the first and last frames of each team shot are identical apart from the players.

**Now:**
- **Every HLAE shot moves:** the script adds up the frames' lengths, the way HLAE's own camera does.
- **Freeze time:** team shots glide round the team (up to 24°) and push in. Where a spawn is too tight for that, the camera pushes straight in instead of standing still. On real spawn positions from pro demos, 79 of 80 team shots move.
- **Camera paths and spawn cameras:** the demo paths fly their whole route, and spawn cameras turn slowly.
- **Pauses:** the glide back and forth until the round goes live works as intended now.

## Team overhead (the names above the players)

Matchframe now sends the three commands you found:
- **`cl_drawhud 1`:** always sent with the game options.
- **`cl_drawhud_force_teamid_overhead`:** goes on and off with **X-ray**, together with `spec_show_xray` and `hud_showtargetid`, so the names also go off in clutches and retakes.
- **Standard launch mode:** the line to paste into the console has all three.

## The HUD freezing

**What your recording shows:** when round 6 went live, the HUD's clock stayed on 1:55 and the minimap's dots stood still for 8 seconds, then everything caught up at once. CS2 ran at 189 fps the whole time. So the HUD got no new game data for 8 seconds; it wasn't too slow to draw.

**What changed:**
- **Matchframe answers CS2 first:** CS2 sends one update at a time and waits for Matchframe's answer. Matchframe now answers before it reads the update, so nothing it does can hold CS2 up.
- **CS2 recovers within a second or two:** if an answer is ever lost, CS2 sends everything again at its next heartbeat, which was every 10 s. It's now every second, with a 1 s timeout. That's the most likely way to get an 8-second gap like yours.
- **The support file says which side stopped:** it now lists every gap of 1.5 s or more in CS2's game data, and every stall of the program, with the round and phase. It already listed the HUD's own drawing stalls.

**One thing to check:** if the HUD's dot in the HUD Library turns amber, the computer drawing the HUD stalled (in OBS: **Settings → Advanced → Sources → Enable Browser Source Hardware Acceleration**).
