# Matchframe Esports 0.10.4-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.10.3:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Replays play back to back, in round order

Before, each automatic replay was its own cut: to the replay scene, one clip, back to the live scene, then to the replay scene again for the next clip. The clips played best first.

**Now, in the break after a round:**
- **One cut in, one cut out:** the program cuts to the replay scene once, plays the round's chosen clips one after another, and cuts back to the live scene after the last.
- **Round order:** the clips play from the start of the round to its end. A clip's place is the moment of its first kill, as this computer saw it in the game data, so clips from the replay PC and this computer's own big plays are in the right order together.
- **Order setting:** under **Automatic replays**, **Order → Best first** brings back the best clip first. Both play back to back.
- **Status:** it reads *Replay 2 of 3: …* while the replays play.

**Between two clips (OBS):**
- The next clip goes into the same media source as soon as the one before ends, so your replay scene stays as it is.
- On air this shows about 0.15 s between clips (one black frame and the next clip's first picture). That was measured with OBS 30.
- OBS starts a clip at the keyframe before its part, so a clip can start a little earlier than planned. That shows as more lead-up, not a gap.

**Between two clips (vMix):** the next clip is added while the one before plays, then vMix cuts to it. Your chosen transition is used to the first replay and back to live after the last.

**Unchanged:**
- **When they play:** replays stop and cut to live at once if the round goes live.
- **Choosing the clips:** the clips are chosen as before, with the same scores and **Replays per break**. They're now chosen together, to fit the break.
- **Clips that don't play:** a clip that doesn't play is skipped and the next one plays.
- **Taking over:** if you change the program yourself, or play a clip by hand, the replays leave it alone.

## Also fixed

**OBS:**
- **Restarting a clip:** restarting the first clip after the cut could undo the skip to its part, so the whole clip played from its start.
- **Clip cut short:** a clip with keyframes far apart could be cut before its kill. The time limit now follows where OBS says the clip is.
- **Clip length:** OBS can report a nonsense length right after loading a file. The program now waits for the real one.

**Playing by hand:** a clip played over another clip on the replay scene now marks the first one as played.
