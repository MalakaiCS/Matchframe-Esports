# Matchframe Esports 0.6.0-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, against a stand-in CS2, in a real OBS 30 on Linux, and in the program itself (Electron). Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.5.3:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Cinematics that hold, and first person when the round starts

What you saw on Mirage — a 1-second van shot repeating five times, and the camera not going back to the players — had several causes, all fixed:

- **The repeating shot:**
  - a camera that CS2 confirmed straight away was never recorded as on air, so the director sent it again and again;
  - the same game data looked at twice in a row also sent the same shot twice.
  - Now a shot plays once a round, whether it worked or not.
- **The 1-second shot:** CS2's game data arrives a moment after the console. Data from before the cut still showed a player, and the director took that to mean the camera had gone back to a player. It now gives a new shot 0.6 s to settle. The data then showing the free camera was also taken as you taking over the camera, which paused the Auto Director and brought it back on another shot. That no longer happens.
- **Freeze time:** at most **two establishing shots**, of the **spawns where the players are** (never an empty spawn). Each plays in full, up to 7 s, and the second shows the other team.
- **Round start:** **first person when the round goes live**, with no overviews or follow cams in the first 10 seconds.
- **Follow cams:** at most **once per map**, about 4.5 s, then back to first person. Smoother: the camera is updated 64 times a second and moves the player on between game-data updates.
- **Nade cams** (Full Action Observer) are on by default and come up more often, especially for executes and flash entries. If you changed Auto Director settings before, check **Nade cams** is ticked.

## kaiHUD's baseboard no longer lags

CS2 sends game data up to 50 times a second, and kaiHUD redrew everything each time. Matchframe now sends an LHM-format HUD the newest data at most 10 times a second, and straight away when something on screen changes: the spectated player, round phase, bomb, who's alive, score. On a slowed-down test machine the baseboard went from 32 to 55 frames a second.

## Create matches, with new teams

**Matches → Create match** is always at the top right. With no teams yet, the form makes them: pick **New team…**, type the name (and a short name, or let it make one).

## Thumbnails and stingers for every match

When you create a match, Matchframe makes these in **Matches → Thumbnails & stingers**:

- **Thumbnails** (1280×720): **Highlights**, **Full replay** and **Live**;
- an **Up next** screen (1920×1080) for OBS before the match;
- a **stinger** and a **replay stinger** (1920×1080 with transparency).

Each uses both teams' logos and colours. A team without a logo gets a **?**. The designs are Matchframe's own, in the spirit of the thumbnails you sent, not copies. **Download** saves each one, **Make again** remakes them after you change logos or the title, and **Background picture** puts a game screenshot behind the thumbnails (or a frame from OBS).

**Stingers in OBS:** create two Stinger transitions once in OBS (Scene Transitions → + → Stinger), named `Matchframe Stinger` and `Matchframe Replay Stinger`, then press **Put the stingers in OBS**. Matchframe:
- loads the videos into them with the cut at 0.9 s;
- uses the replay one for replays;
- makes the plain one OBS's transition, if you leave that ticked.

Activating a match switches the stingers to that match's.

Step by step: [Matches, thumbnails and stingers](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/blob/v0.6.0/docs/operations/match-graphics.md).

## Also fixed

- **Replay transition:** OBS changes its transition a moment after it is asked, so a replay could be cut with the transition from before (the Stinger chosen for replays was sometimes skipped). Matchframe now waits until OBS has switched.

## Checked automatically

- **Two Mirage rounds** against a stand-in CS2 whose game data lags its console by 0.25 s:
  - spawn shots held in full, once each;
  - first person at live;
  - without the fixes, the shot was cut after 0.4 s.
- **Follow cam:** once per map, handed back after 4–5 s, steady steps.
- **kaiHUD's baseboard:** fewer updates and higher frame rate, measured in a browser.
- **Matches:**
  - a match created from no teams;
  - all six graphics made by the program itself (Chromium and Electron), with the stingers' transparency read back by FFmpeg's decoder.
- **Real OBS 30 (Linux):**
  - both stinger transitions set and confirmed;
  - recordings show the scenes through the stinger and never the cut.
