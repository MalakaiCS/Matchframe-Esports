# Matchframe Esports 0.6.1-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, against a stand-in CS2 whose game data arrives late like the real game's. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.6.0:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Nothing to change: a wrong camera setting saved by 0.6.0 repairs itself on the first freeze-time shot.

## Back to the players after the freeze-time shots

What your Dust2 recording showed: the second freeze-time shot (the Ts in tunnels) stayed on air for 35 s, through freeze time and 12 s into the live round, while five players died off screen.

Matchframe kept trying to go to a player every 1.6 s, but CS2 never left the free camera. CS2's game data arrives a moment after its console has acted, and Matchframe read it too early, in two places:

- **Right after a camera shot,** game data from before the shot still showed a player. Matchframe took that to mean the camera was already back on a player, and sent the next switch without the command that leaves the free camera.
- **After leaving the free camera,** Matchframe checked 0.3 s later whether it worked. The late game data still said "free camera", so Matchframe decided this CS2 numbers its camera modes the other way, switched to that and saved it. From then on every attempt to leave a cinematic shot failed.

Now:

- after a camera shot, the camera counts as free whatever older game data says, so the switch always includes the command that leaves it;
- the other numbering is tried only after the game data has shown the free camera for 2 s, and Matchframe keeps whichever one the game data confirms. A wrong numbering saved by 0.6.0 is put right the same way.

## CS2 keeps running behind other windows

In the recording, CS2's picture and sound stopped for 8 s, then jumped 9 s on. CS2 does that in full screen when another window, such as Matchframe or OBS, takes focus. It also goes silent and slows down behind other windows.

**HUD Library → Game options → Run behind other windows** (on by default with the console launch modes) now keeps CS2 drawing every frame and playing its sound when another window has focus (`engine_no_focus_sleep 0`, `snd_mute_losefocus 0`). Also set CS2's display mode to **Fullscreen Windowed** on the observer computer: in plain Fullscreen, clicking another window still stops the picture.

The **Esc menu** in the recording (at 0:18 and 1:45) is CS2's own and opens when Esc is pressed in CS2. Matchframe never sends Esc.

## A record of what the camera was told

When a shot looks wrong, `%APPDATA%\Matchframe Esports\logs\director.log` now shows what Matchframe sent to CS2's console, what each command did, what the director decided and why, and what the game data showed (round, phase, spectated player, free camera). **Settings → Diagnostics → Download support file** includes the latest part of it. Send it with a recording and the cause can be found from what actually happened.
