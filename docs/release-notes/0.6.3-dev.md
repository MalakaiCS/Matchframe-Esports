# Matchframe Esports 0.6.3-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, against CS2's own map geometry and the real player positions from a pro match on each map. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.6.2:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Nothing to change.

## Team shots, checked on real spawns

Thanks to the demos you uploaded, there's now one pro match for each map in the pool: Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage and Nuke. From every round of each match, Matchframe read where every player stood as freeze time ended, and placed a team shot for both teams:

- **Every team in every round gets a shot:** 272 in all.
- **All five players in the shot in 263.** In the other nine, one player stood well away from their team and the shot frames the other four at a good size:
  - six Dust II rounds;
  - three Mirage rounds, where the Ts stand in a line along a narrow street.
- **Checked against CS2's own geometry:** along each camera's whole glide, it stays in open space and sees the players.

The camera now also considers how big the players look in the picture, with the middle player about 380 units from the camera. A good-sized shot of four beats a distant shot of five.

## Spawn cameras aim at the real spawns

The map data's spawn areas came from the old area names. Their middles were 100 to 500 units from where players really spawn, and on Ancient and Anubis the CT spawn area missed the spawns altogether. Each map's spawn areas are now where players stood in the demos, and the spawn cameras were placed again to look there. No other camera moved.

## Cache

Cache had no map data. It now has, like the other maps:

- its areas, from the pro demo (46, with both sites and both spawns);
- a free-space grid;
- 87 cameras and 10 camera paths, checked against CS2's own geometry.

The default map pool is now the seven pool maps. Overpass, Train and Vertigo still work, outside the default pool.
