# Matchframe Esports 0.8.1-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Fixed from your 0.8.0 support file; not yet seen in a real CS2.**

**Coming from 0.8.0:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Cinematics no longer flicker, and they move

**The flicker in pauses and freeze time (fixed):**
- **Why it happened:** while HLAE draws a cinematic, CS2 keeps reporting the player it showed before the cut. Matchframe took that as "back on the player" after 0.6 s.
- **In a pause:** it sent the same shot again every second or two. Each time CS2 switched spectator mode underneath, which was the flicker.
- **In freeze time:** it started a new shot about every 3 s.
- **Now:** a cinematic stays on air for its whole length. It ends when the director cuts away, or when CS2 really moves to another player (you press a number key in CS2, or the player dies); HLAE then shows that player.

**Moving shots:**
- **Team shots:** in freeze time they glide round the team about 24° and push in closer (they moved only 10° before, which looked still). Paths now play to the end instead of being cut after 3 s.
- **Pauses:** the shot of the team that called the timeout now glides round them, one way for 20 s and then back, until the round goes live. It was a still picture before.

## Also fixed

- **The demo-trained POV model** (built in since 0.7.0) didn't load on Windows: the build's line endings changed the file, so its checksum didn't match. It loads now, and the director uses what the demos taught it again.
- **Repeated player selection:** after cutting from a cinematic to a player, Matchframe no longer sends the same player selection 17 times in half a second.
