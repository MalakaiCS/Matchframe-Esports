# Matchframe Esports 0.7.2-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. The new camera paths were checked against CS2's collision geometry, not yet seen inside a real CS2.**

**Coming from 0.7.1:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. The new paths play while CS2 runs in the **Observer tools + HLAE** mode.

## 200–300 camera paths on each map, from your demos

As you asked: camera paths built from the demos you provided, where players really walked and fought.

| Map | Paths from demos |

- **Kinds of shot:**
  - **Orbits** round the places where fights happened, bombs were planted and teams stand in freeze time.
  - **Push-ins, pull-outs, cranes and slides** towards, away from, up over and across those places.
  - **Routes** that fly along the ways players really ran (out of spawn, rotations, entries), a little behind and above, looking ahead.
- **Checked against CS2's geometry:** every path was flown through the map's collision grid before it was kept. Each one has room round the camera, never passes through a wall or floor, and has no wall filling the picture. It stays over the playable area, keeps the place in view, and moves and turns gently.
- **Where they play:** in freeze time, half time and at the end of the map, alongside the team shots and spawn cameras. Only shots that show at least two players are used. Equally good shots take turns from round to round, so freeze times look different.
- **Only with the HLAE camera:** paths play while HLAE is connected, because over console commands they judder. Without HLAE, freeze time stays as in 0.7.1.
- **Names:** each path is named after its area, for example "Orbit: B site 3" or "Route: T spawn to Outside Long (T)".
- **Switching off:** they are built-in paths, so Maps & Cameras → **Built-in cameras** switches them off with the others.

## Not verified yet

- **In a real CS2.** Please start CS2 with Observer tools + HLAE and watch a few freeze times and a half time. Send the support file with a recording of any path that clips into something or looks wrong, and say its name. Glass, fences and foliage are not in CS2's collision geometry, so a path can pass close to them.
- **One demo per map so far.** More demos of a map give more routes and fight spots.
- **Overpass, Train and Vertigo** have no demos yet, so they keep their 9–10 built-in paths.
