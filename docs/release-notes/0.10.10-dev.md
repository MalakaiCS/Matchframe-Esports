**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.10.9:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Lineup updates to 1.2.1, and your settings for it stay.

## Lineup: the plant countdown

While the bomb is being planted, Lineup's panel under the score bar ("… IS PLANTING THE BOMB") now counts down the plant itself, from 3.20 to the moment the bomb is down, with its bar draining. Before, it showed the round clock. It uses the game's own plant countdown when the game sends one.

After the plant, nothing changes: the bomb sign and the 40-second bar.
