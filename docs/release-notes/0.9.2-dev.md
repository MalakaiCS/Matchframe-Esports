# Matchframe Esports 0.9.2-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, with kaiHUD 1.0.2 measured in Chromium. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.9.1:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## kaiHUD no longer falls behind

Measured with kaiHUD and busy game data (molotovs, smokes, full loadouts):

| HUD (same game data) | How busy its page is |
|---|---|
| Matchframe's own HUD | 12 % |
| kaiHUD | 31 % |

kaiHUD redraws much of its page for every game update, so it does about 2.6 times the work. On a PC also running CS2 (and OBS), the other HUDs have room to spare and kaiHUD runs out first. 0.9.1 doubled its updates, which made that worse.

Now each HUD output checks whether its page is keeping up. When kaiHUD's page falls behind, Matchframe gives kaiHUD fewer updates: 20 a second, then 10, then 5. Once the page has had room for a minute, it goes back up. When your PC has room, kaiHUD keeps 20 a second and the smooth minimap.

On a test PC slowed down 4 times, kaiHUD drew 42 frames a second at 20 updates, 47 at 10, and about 54 at 5.

## Anubis on kaiHUD's minimap

kaiHUD has minimaps for nine maps, not Anubis. For other maps it asks the program running it. Matchframe now answers with an Anubis minimap drawn from where players stand in pro games, in kaiHUD's style: dark map, red bombsites, A and B. It's blockier than kaiHUD's own drawings, but the players land in the right places.
