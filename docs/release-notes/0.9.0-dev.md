# Matchframe Esports 0.9.0-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner; the replay feed with a stand-in NDI source, vMix with a stand-in. Not yet seen with real NDI, vMix or CS2.**

**Coming from 0.8.2:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded, on both computers.

## The best replays of each round

- **The replay PC films every kill it can.** It sits behind the main PC (it goes back and stays behind on every map) and gets on each killer before the kill plays there. That now includes the kills the main camera showed, not only the ones it missed. Set this under **Replay PC → Kills to film**.
- **The main PC ranks each round's clips, best to worst.** Under **Automatic replays** you see each clip's score and why. Points come from:
  - kills (a 3K is worth far more than three single kills) and a won clutch;
  - not seen live, or another angle;
  - knife, Zeus, Deagle or AWP, and headshots;
  - the opening kill, the kill that won the round, and a fast multi-kill.
- **It replays the best 3–4 and leaves the rest.** **Replays per break** defaults to the best 4 that fit, best first. A clip that doesn't quite fit has its lead-up shortened, down to 2 s before the kill. Two clips of the same kills count once.
- **It waits for the replay PC's last kills.** The replay PC plays the round a few seconds later, and a round's last kills are often its best. The first replay waits for them, up to 8 s.

## Replay feed (NDI), like LHM's

The main PC can record the replay PC's whole screen over NDI and cut every kill from that recording. The replay PC then needs no OBS.

**On the replay PC:**
1. Install the NDI runtime: http://ndi.link/NDIRedistV6.
2. Run vMix Desktop Capture NDI: https://cdn.vmix.com/download/vMixDesktopCaptureNDI.zip, extracted.
3. Connect to the delayed CSTV and set Matchframe up as the replay PC.

**On the main PC:** Replays & Production → Replay PC → **This is the main PC** → **Replay feed (NDI)**:
1. **Download ffmpeg** (a 92 MB download, checked before use). It encodes the recording with your graphics card's encoder, such as NVENC on your RTX 4060.
2. **Find NDI sources** and choose the replay PC's.
3. Tick **Record the replay PC's screen.**

It keeps the last 4 minutes. NDI at 1080p60 needs about 100–150 Mbit/s, so connect the two computers on a wired gigabit network.

## Replays in vMix

**Automatic replays → Play replays in → vMix.** Turn on vMix's Web Controller (vMix → Settings → Web Controller) and press **Test vMix**. For each replay:
- it's added to vMix as an input, started at the part to show, and transitioned to (Cut, Fade, Stinger 1 or Stinger 2);
- when it ends, vMix goes back to the input that was on air, and the replay input is removed.

OBS works as before.
