# MatchFrame Esports 0.13.1-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.13.0:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

**On event day:** untick **Settings → General → Program → Download updates automatically**, so a newer version doesn't install if you restart the program during the show. Turn it back on afterwards.

## Early replays when the round is decided

Automatic replays can start up to 10 seconds before the round ends, when the round is already decided:
- **Bomb planted:** no CT can reach the bomb and defuse it in time. That's worked out from each CT's walk along the map's routes, plus 5 s with a kit or 10 s without. A CT who could still sneak in and defuse keeps the round open.
- **Ts saving:** no T can get the bomb to a site and plant before the round timer runs out.

**When they never start:**
- **Fights:** a CT and a T close to each other (about 800 units of walking), or with a sightline opening, or hunting each other.
- **The 10-second rule:** never with more than 10 seconds left on the bomb timer (bomb planted) or the round timer (Ts saving).
- **Other things on air:** a map intro, highlights, a timeout or pause, or your own OBS scene.

**Holding and timing:**
- The round has to stay decided for 1.5 seconds first, and a kill starts that wait again.
- With under 3 seconds left, replays just wait for the normal round end.

**Back to live:** if the round heats up while early replays are on air, the program cuts straight back to the game. That means a CT back in reach of the bomb, a fight starting, a kill, or a T in reach of a plant. The round's remaining clips then play when it ends.

**Clips:** the replay PC runs behind live, so clips it hasn't filmed yet join the break when there's room.

**Live Control** shows why, for example "Round decided: no CT can defuse (fuse 0:08; nearest CT 1,900 u, no kit). Replays in 2 s."

**Settings** in Automatic replays:
- **Start replays early when the round is decided** (on);
- **At most … seconds early** (5–10);
- **Back to live if the round isn't over after all** (on).

**Not counted:** smokes, molotovs, ladders and boosts. **Not yet tried** in a real CS2 match.

## Stage HUD: the round MVP card

**Timing:**
- **In:** the card comes in **5 seconds into freeze time**. The player's card grows into the MVP card in about half a second.
- **Out:** it goes back into the player's card after the new setting **Hide the round MVP after … seconds of the round** (5–10, default 8).
- **Not shown:** at round end, in warmup, during a timeout or pause, or while the bomb is planted.
- **Page opened mid-freeze:** a HUD page opened part-way through freeze time can show the card up to about 2 seconds early, because it can't see how long the freeze time is.

**Low-spec mode** uses a quick wipe instead.
