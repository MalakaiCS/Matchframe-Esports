# Matchframe Esports 0.1.3-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Installed and smoke-tested on a Windows CI runner; not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

## What's new: a cleaner program

Every screen in the program has been tidied up. Nothing was removed: rarely used settings and technical detail are folded away under **Details**, **Options**, **Advanced** or **How this works**, and explanations moved into tooltips.

- **Top bar:** four compact status pills instead of eight boxes: connection and game data, the Observer and who owns the camera (with Resume automation or Take over), auto-director hours and OBS. Hover a pill for the detail. **Stop automation** (F8) stays on the right.
- **Sidebar:** grouped into Broadcast, Event, Graphics & cameras, Analysis and System, with shorter names. Setup now lives under System.
- **Calmer look:** headings and labels in normal sentence case instead of capitals, fewer lines and boxes, quieter badges and notes. A disabled button explains why when you hover it, instead of a warning under every button. Forms for new events, teams, players, matches, sponsors and cameras open from a **New…** or **Add…** button.
- **Overview:** the setup steps still to do, a short status list and alerts.
- **Live Control:** a slim match strip; one **Camera** panel with what is on air, what the director wants and any pending command; one **Observer** panel with the mode picker, Follow action, Return live, Hold, Pin and Cinematic. Cinematics, one-side and replay options are under **Options**. The second Stop automation button on this screen is gone: the one in the top bar is always there.
- **Setup:** four groups (Game data, Camera control, OBS, Profiles) instead of twelve panels, about half the length.
- **Maps & Cameras:** a tab for maps and cameras and a tab for utility coverage; one panel per map; a simpler camera table. About a third of the old length.
- **Settings:** tabs with the everyday settings first (General, Stats & reports, Diagnostics, Logs, Audit log, Advanced).
- **Director, HUDs, Sponsors, Studio, Replays, Player Cameras, Demos, Training, Devices, Events, Teams, Matches and Account:** fewer panels, shorter text, and per-row details folded away.

## New logo

- The **Matchframe Esports logo** is in the program: the mark in the top bar, the badge on the sign-in screen, and the program icon for the `.exe`, the installer, the Start menu and taskbar.
- On the website: the mark in the header and footer, new favicons, and a preview image when the website is shared. The website's colours now match the logo's blues, and its product screenshots show the new, cleaner program.
