# Matchframe Esports 0.1.3-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Installed and smoke-tested on a Windows CI runner; not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

## What's new: a cleaner program

Every screen in the program has been tidied up. Nothing was removed: rarely used settings and technical detail are folded away under **Details**, **Options** or **Advanced** disclosures, and explanations moved into tooltips.

- **Top bar:** four compact status pills instead of eight boxes: connection and game data, Observer and camera owner (with Resume automation or Take over), auto-director hours and OBS. Hover a pill for the detail. **Stop automation** (F8) stays on the right.
- **Sidebar:** grouped into Broadcast, Event, Graphics & cameras, Analysis and System, with shorter names. Setup now lives under System.
- **Calmer look:** headings and labels are in normal sentence case instead of capitals, panels have fewer lines and boxes, and badges and notes are quieter. A disabled button explains why when you hover it, instead of a warning under every button.
- **Overview:** the setup steps still to do, a short status list and alerts.
- **Live Control:** a slim match strip; one **Camera** panel showing what is on air, what the director wants and any pending command; one **Observer** panel with the mode picker, Follow action, Return live, Hold, Pin and Cinematic. Cinematics, one-side and replay options are under **Options**. The second Stop automation button on this screen is gone, because the one in the top bar is always there.
