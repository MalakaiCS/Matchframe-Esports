# Matchframe Esports 0.5.1-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, against a stand-in CS2 and with a real OBS on Linux. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.5.0:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Update both computers: the main PC must be on 0.5.1 for the replay PC's new modes.

## A replay PC that knows the kill is coming

Run the replay PC's game a few seconds **behind** the main PC's, and every kill the main camera misses is known on the replay PC before it happens there. The replay PC's camera gets on the killer a few seconds before the kill, films it, and sends it to the main PC. Nothing has to be guessed.

The replay PC works out by itself how far behind it is (from the two round clocks and the kills both see). **Replays & Production → Replay PC** shows it, for example *7.0 s behind*.

How to get the replay PC behind:

- **Main PC in the server as a spectator:** the replay PC watches the server's CSTV. A CSTV delay of about 7 s is best.
- **Main PC on CSTV:** give the server a second CSTV, 7 s later, and connect the replay PC to it. With the main CSTV at `tv_delay 10`:

  ```
  tv_enable1 1
  tv_port1 27021
  tv_delay1 17
  ```

  The main PC connects to `server:27020`, the replay PC to `server:27021`. The panel has these lines with a Copy button.

**Two missed kills at once:** the replay PC films the better one: more kills first, then a missed kill over another angle. If the other player gets a second kill, it switches to that 2K.

## Rewinding, when both watch the same CSTV (experimental)

With the replay PC at the same time as the main PC, tick **Rewind to film a missed kill**:

1. A kill happens that the main camera missed, and the replay PC's camera didn't have the killer.
2. CS2 goes back 3–12 s, far enough for the kill's lead-up.
3. The replay PC films the kill from the killer and sends it.
4. CS2 skips forward to live again.

**Only a CSTV+ stream (`playcast`) may be able to go back.** A normal CSTV connection (`connect ip:port`) can't: CS2's instant-replay commands are developer-only. The program detects this, says so in the panel, and the replay PC keeps filming someone else as in 0.5.0. Going back on a CSTV+ stream comes from CS2's own command list, but hasn't been tried on a real stream yet.

## Also

- **Better judging at the same time:** the main PC now judges each of its kills with its exact camera history (killer shown, only the victim, or neither). The replay PC uses that to decide whether a clip is a missed kill, another angle, or not needed.
- **What the panel shows:** what the replay PC is filming now, and the kills it couldn't film, with the reason.

Step by step: [Replay PC and automatic replays](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/blob/v0.5.1/docs/operations/replay-pc.md).

## Checked automatically

- **Two programs, the replay PC 3 s behind:** it measured 3 s, was on the killer before the kill played there, and sent the clip from 2 s before to 0.5 s after the kill.
- **A single kill and a 2K at the same time:** the 2K was filmed, even after the replay PC had started on the single kill.
- **Rewinding with a stand-in CS2 console:**
  - it went back, filmed the kill with its whole lead-up, and came forward to live;
  - a console that refuses was reported as unsupported.
- **The delay measurement:** behind, at the same time, ahead, a paused clock, a rewind and a skip forward, kills only, and readings that disagree.
- **0.5.0's replay PC checks** still pass, including against a real OBS (Linux, Xvfb).
- **The installed program on a Windows runner:** the same checks as 0.5.0.
