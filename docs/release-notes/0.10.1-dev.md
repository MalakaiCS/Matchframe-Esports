# Matchframe Esports 0.10.1-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen in CS2 on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.10.0:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Series boxes in Vector and Lineup

The small boxes next to each team's score are the series score now:
- **How many:** best of 1 shows 1 per side, best of 3 shows 2, best of 5 shows 3, and best of 7 shows 5.
- **Filling:** a box fills when that team wins a map.
- **Where:** in Lineup they're between the score and the logo; in Vector, in the capsule at each end of the score bar. Vector no longer shows timeouts there.

## HUD images: your pictures in every Matchframe HUD

**HUD Library → HUD images** has places for your own pictures:
- **Player pictures:** 5 for CT and 5 for T, used for players without their own photo. A team's first player takes picture 1, and so on.
- **Maps:** a logo and a picture for each map, shown on the series and veto tiles.
- **Dead player icon:** shown in place of the HUD's own mark.

**Which HUDs:** Vector, Lineup, the Pro CS2 HUD and the built-in Broadcast HUD all use them. Without pictures, they look as before.

**Which images:** add only images you have the right to use.

**Each computer:** HUD images aren't shared between computers yet; add them on each one.

## The same radars in every HUD

Every Matchframe HUD uses the radars you picked: the navy ones with white walls and red bomb sites that come with Matchframe, for Ancient, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage, Nuke, Overpass, Train and Vertigo.

**Anubis and Cache:** these have no such radar, so Vector, Lineup, the Pro CS2 HUD and the Broadcast HUD show one Matchframe draws in the same style (before, an outline). Players, the bomb and utility land where they should.

**Your own radar:** for any map, calibrate one in Maps & Cameras and every HUD uses it.

## Lineup: round damage

A dead player's card shows **ROUND DAMAGE** like your reference, when CS2 reports it.
