## Replays and highlights: one scene, smooth fades, sound that fades out

**The problem:** your OBS on Windows doesn't keep a clip paused while it waits off air. The next clip played on in the background, and after MatchFrame pulled it back it sometimes wouldn't start again: it froze, then faded to the clip after it.

**The new default: one video per break.** As soon as a break's clips are chosen, MatchFrame joins them into one video with your graphics card's encoder:
- each clip from its lead-up through its play;
- each fading into the next, picture and sound together;
- the sound fading in at the start and out at the end, so there's no hard cut.

OBS plays that one video in your one replay scene. Nothing has to be paused or started between clips.

**What stays the same:**
- **Replay graphic:** it follows the video, changing at each clip.
- **Status:** it reads *Replay 2 of 3*.
- **Controls:** **Skip** jumps to the next clip; **Hold** pauses.
- **Stopping:** **Stop** and the 1:50 cut back fade the sound out first.
- **Exclude and Pin during a break:** they apply from the next break, because that break's video is already made.
- **Highlights on air and Test replays:** they work the same way. Highlights show *Preparing highlights… n%* while the video is made.
- **The "B" copy of your replay scene:** it's no longer needed in this mode. You can remove it, or keep it for the fallback below.

**If the video isn't ready in time** (over 6 seconds), fails, or OBS won't play it, the break plays the clips through the two replay scenes instead, and the panel says why. That way has been fixed for your OBS too:
- the next clip is loaded only just before its fade, never paused;
- every clip is checked to be moving on air. If it isn't, MatchFrame sends play again, then reloads it, before skipping it.

**Setting:** **Between replays** offers:
- **Fade (one scene, rendered)**, the default;
- **Fade (two scenes, OBS)**;
- **Cut**.

There's also **Sound fade** for the length of the sound fades.

**Check:** after a break, the Automatic replays panel shows how long its video took to make. If it's often over 6 seconds, send me a support file.

## Replay feed: no more restarts

When the encoder falls behind for a moment, the recording of the replay PC now drops a few frames instead of starting again, which used to leave a gap. It starts again only if ffmpeg stops for 5 seconds.
