**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, against a stand-in CS2 and with a real OBS on Linux. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.5.1:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Update both computers.

## Same CSTV, 7 seconds behind

On a live CSTV connection CS2 can go back: `demo_gototick -448` goes back 7 s (64 ticks a second). Thanks for the tip. 0.5.1 wrongly said only CSTV+ streams could.

So the replay PC no longer needs a second CSTV to stay behind the main PC:

- **Going back once:** when it finds itself at the main PC's time (both on the same CSTV), CS2 goes back 7 s once and keeps playing from there.
- **What that gives you:** every kill the main camera misses is then known on the replay PC before it happens there. Its camera gets on the killer first and films the whole kill.
- **Checking:** the replay PC checks the jump in its round clock and shows *7.0 s behind* in **Replays & Production → Replay PC**.
- **Staying there:** if a reconnect or a map change brings it back to the main PC's time, it goes back again by itself.

**On the same CSTV as the main PC** has three choices:

- **Go back and stay behind** (the default): 5, 7 or 10 s.
- **Go back for each missed kill, then live:** what you described. CS2 goes back far enough for the kill's lead-up (`demo_gototick -N`), the replay PC films it, then skips forward (`demo_gototick N 1`). If CS2 doesn't skip forward, the replay PC simply stays behind and keeps working from there.
- **Don't go back:** films someone else, as in 0.5.0.

Connected straight to the game server instead of CSTV, CS2 can't go back. The panel says so and the replay PC films someone else.

If you used 0.5.1's rewind switch, it becomes **Go back for each missed kill**.

## Fixed

- **Measuring right after going back:** right after linking, going back was wrongly judged to have failed. After the jump, the replay PC's round clock showed moments the main PC had reported before the two were linked, so the delay couldn't be measured for a few seconds. The jump itself now gives the new delay at once.
- **The command:** going back is sent exactly as it works by hand: `demo_gototick -448`, with no extra argument.

Step by step: [Replay PC and automatic replays](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/blob/v0.5.2/docs/operations/replay-pc.md).
