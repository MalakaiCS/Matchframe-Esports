# Matchframe Esports 0.4.2-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Installed and checked on a Windows CI runner. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.4.1:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Everything in [0.4.0](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/releases/tag/v0.4.0) and [0.4.1](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/releases/tag/v0.4.1) is included.

## Cameras checked against CS2's own geometry

The built-in cameras and camera paths are rebuilt from CS2's navigation meshes and collision meshes for the current CS2 build (1.41.9.2, 22 October). This is the same geometry players collide with.

- **The old cameras had real problems:** tested against this geometry, 30 of the 92 cameras in 0.4.0 and 0.4.1 either looked through a wall at their target or sat inside a wall or prop.
- **Every new camera passes these checks:**
  - it has clear space around it;
  - it sees its area's centre and at least half the area (86 % on a typical camera);
  - most of its view is open, with no wall right in front of the lens;
  - it aims at the middle of what it can see.
- **Every path passes too:** the curve the camera actually flies is clear of walls the whole way and keeps the area in view.
- **104 cameras and 81 paths** on Ancient, Anubis, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage, Nuke, Overpass, Train and Vertigo:

  | Map | Cameras | Paths |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ancient | 12 | 10 |
  | Anubis | 12 | 9 |
  | Dust II | 12 | 8 |
  | Inferno | 12 | 9 |
  | Mirage | 12 | 10 |
  | Nuke | 12 | 10 |
  | Overpass | 9 | 7 |
  | Train | 11 | 9 |
  | Vertigo | 12 | 9 |

- **Paths per area:** each area gets orbits, push-ins and slow sideways drifts. Orbits are at most 10 seconds, so they fit in freeze time.
- **Filled in:** Train CT spawn, Vertigo T spawn, and a mid camera on Nuke (Outside), Train (Connector) and Overpass (Connector).
- **Still missing:** Overpass CT spawn (no data names that area) and a moving shot for Dust II CT spawn.

### What the checks can't see

- **See-through things:** collision geometry leaves out glass, fences, foliage and other props you can see through or walk through. So a shot can still show one of those in front of the action.
- **Not seen by eye:** nobody has looked through these cameras in CS2 yet.

If a camera looks wrong, mark it **Invalidated** in **Maps & Cameras** and it's never used again, or switch **Built-in cameras** off there.

## Checked automatically

- **Geometry checks:** every built-in camera and path passes the clearance and line-of-sight checks against the CS2 meshes. I also looked at rough ray-traced previews of what each camera sees.
- **Map packs:** all nine validate against the map-pack schema. A new test checks that every map has its built-in cameras and paths, three cameras per bombsite, and paths that fit in freeze time.
- **The installed program on a Windows runner:** the same checks as 0.4.0.
