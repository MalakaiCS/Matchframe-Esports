## Kill feed: the round's last kill shows

**Before:** the kill that ended a round often never showed in Matchframe's HUDs (Vector, Lineup, Pro CS2 and the Broadcast HUD). The whole kill feed vanished the moment the round ended.

**Why:** CS2 moves its round counter on in the same game update that brings that kill. Matchframe cleared the kill feed for the "new" round in that update, right after adding the kill.

**Now:** the kill feed stays up through the end of the round, the round-winning kill included, and clears when the next round starts (freeze time), as CS2's own does.
