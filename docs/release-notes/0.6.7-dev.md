# Matchframe Esports 0.6.7-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, against a stand-in CS2 console that now switches to the free camera a tick late, like CS2. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.6.6:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Nothing to change.

## Fixed: cinematics stuck in the ground under the player

When a cinematic started, CS2 went into the free camera but stayed in the ground under the player instead of moving to the camera.

- **The cause:** Matchframe sent "free camera" and "go to this spot" (`spec_goto`) on one line. CS2 switches to the free camera a tick later, so the "go to" arrived while the camera still followed the player, and CS2 dropped it. The free camera then started where the player stands: in the ground under them. This is also what the earlier Mirage report was. 0.6.2 fixed the sliding camera but not this.
- **Now:** the position is sent again 50, 150 and 300 ms after the switch, and Matchframe checks where the camera actually is (`getpos`). If it is still elsewhere, the position is sent once more.
- **A camera that still didn't move** is now reported as failed, and the director goes straight back to a player. It is never left in the free camera in the ground. The director log records where `getpos` said the camera was.
- The test stand-in for CS2 now switches to the free camera a tick late and starts it at the player's feet. 0.6.6 fails the freeze-time test against it the way you saw; 0.6.7 passes.
