# Matchframe Esports 0.8.0-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Player cams were tested end to end in Chromium with a fake camera, not yet with players in a real match.**

**Coming from 0.7.2:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. **The Matchframe website must be redeployed with this version** for the player cam page to exist there.

## Player cams

As you asked, the way LHM does it: players send their face cam from a page on the Matchframe website, and you show it in the broadcast, delayed as long as you need.

**For players:** they open the link you send them. The page shows the match being played. They pick their name, pick their camera, see a preview, and press **Approve and send**. The page then tells them whether their cam is live. They can put the game on top and keep the page open (Chrome or Edge).

**For you** (**Cameras → Player cameras**):
- Switch **Player cams** on. You need to be signed in to your Matchframe account. Copy the link and send it to the players. **New link** stops the old one.
- **Delay:** set it to your CSTV delay, up to 300 s, so faces match the game on screen.
- **On air:**
  - each player's cam appears with a live preview, its camera, size and bitrate;
  - cams go on air as soon as they arrive, or switch that off and put each one on air yourself;
  - **Take off air** hides a cam; **Stop camera** sends the player away.
- **Video:** Low, Standard (640×360, about 0.7 Mbit/s a player) or High.

**Where they show:**
- **LHM HUDs** (such as kaiHUD): through their own player-cam feature, with no setup. Reload a HUD that was already open.
- **Matchframe's own HUD:** above the observed player's panel.
- **OBS:** a browser source with the observed player's cam (transparent when they have none), or one for a single player. Copy the addresses from the screen.

The video goes through the Matchframe website to your program and isn't stored. Guide: `docs/integrations/player-cams.md`.
