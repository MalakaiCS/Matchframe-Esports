# Matchframe Esports 0.10.0-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen in CS2 on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.9.5:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. The two new HUDs appear in the HUD Library after the restart.

## Fixed: the free camera under a player after cinematics

When a cinematic ended, CS2 sometimes stayed in the free camera under a player instead of going to a player's view. Your support file showed why.

Matchframe had learned the wrong first-person command, `spec_mode 4`, which on your CS2 is the free camera. It learned it from the game data, and on your CS2 the game data names a player even while the free camera is on. After that, every return from a cinematic put the free camera at a player's feet. The retries only moved it from one player to the next.

Now Matchframe checks where the camera really is (`getpos`): at the player's eyes (first person) or at their feet (still the free camera). If it's still free, it tries the other first-person command and keeps the one that works. The game data no longer decides this.

Your PC has the wrong command saved. It corrects itself the first time a cinematic ends after the update: you may see the free camera for about half a second, once.

## Two new HUDs: Vector and Lineup

Two broadcast HUDs of Matchframe's own, built from the two HUDs you sent. They match those HUDs closely: layout, sizes, colours and animations, measured frame by frame from your recordings. They use none of the originals' artwork, logos or code. Players without a photo show a silhouette, and teams without a logo show their short name.

### Vector

Each team's players run down its own side of the screen.
- **Player cards:** a photo, a team-colour band with the name and health, and a dark area with K/D, money, weapon and utility.
- **Score bar:** solid blue and red halves with the clock between them.

- **Freeze time:** the loss bonus hangs off both ends of the score bar, the series maps sit under it, and each team's grenades and equipment value sit above its players.
- **Going live:** the clock flips, the loss bonus tucks into the bar, the series wipes away, and the players-alive box slides in from the right.
- **A kill:** the victim's card collapses to a narrow dark card, then the kill slides into the killfeed.
- **Bomb:** the clock turns into the bomb and a bar drops in under the score bar and drains. While the bomb is defused the bar turns blue, with "bomb being defused" under it.
- **Round won:** the winner's card fades in under the score bar.

### Lineup

All ten players in one row along the bottom, with the observed player in the middle.
- **Player cards:** small solid blue and red cards, with each player's photo standing above their card.
- **Score bar:** slim and dark, with team-colour ends.

- **Freeze time:** the middle panel shows both teams' equipment, money, loss bonus and grenades. When the round goes live it gives way to the observed player.
- **Round won:** the winner's colour sweeps out from the middle of the score bar over the whole bar, then their logo comes in. The bar goes back to normal, with the new score, when freeze time starts.
- **A kill:** the card turns grey and drops slightly, then the kill slides into the killfeed.
- **Top left:** an event title and the series maps. **Top right:** a sponsor slot (your scheduled sponsor, or the text you set) above the killfeed.

### Both HUDs

- They show the radar, killfeed, observed player, health bars with a damage trail, money, utility, bomb and defuse kit.
- They have a **compact** variant without the radar and freeze-time extras.
- **Appearance** sets the colours and which parts show.
- Built to stay smooth: only movement and fading animate, and only the clocks update ten times a second.

**To use one:** HUD Library → **Vector HUD** or **Lineup HUD** → **Desktop** switch, **Preview**, or **Copy OBS Browser URL**.

**Not shown:** two things in the originals aren't in CS2's game data, so they're left out.
- Damage this round on a dead player's card. Lineup shows kills this round instead.
- How far a defuse has got. The bar keeps showing the bomb's time.
