# Matchframe Esports 0.6.5-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, against a stand-in CS2 console. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.6.4:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Nothing to change.

## No X-ray in retakes

After the bomb is planted, X-ray now also goes off for a retake, until the round is over. Viewers don't see where the Ts are waiting, just as the CTs don't.

It goes off only when a retake is really on:

- **The CTs are going for it:** a CT is within 1,600 units of the bomb (about a site's approach), or one is defusing. CTs saving far away keep X-ray on.
- **There is time to defuse:** 5 s left with a kit, 10 s without. A lost cause keeps X-ray on.
- **Someone is left to hide:** both teams have players alive. If the last T is dead, it's just a defuse.

It's on by default: **HUD Library → Game options → No X-ray in retakes**. X-ray comes back on for the next round. A 1v2 or bigger after the plant counts as a clutch, as in 0.6.4.

## Follow cams removed

The Auto Director no longer flies a free camera behind running players. It shows:

- first person;
- cinematic cameras: the freeze-time team shots and spawn cameras, the bomb, fight overviews;
- nade cams.

The **Follow cams** switch is gone from the Auto Director card. The third-person view while a player plants or defuses and when their team wins the round is still there: turn off **Chase cam** on the card for first person only.
