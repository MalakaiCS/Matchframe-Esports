## Teams on the right side

**Swap teams** now works whenever a match is active, however MatchFrame placed the teams. You can use it from:
- the button in Live Control;
- **Ctrl+Shift+F11** in the console;
- a global hotkey that works with CS2 or OBS in front;
- the Stream Deck / Companion action `teams_swap`.

The global hotkey is off until you set it: **Live Control → Swap teams hotkey and Stream Deck → Use the suggested keys**. A swap stays for that match and map, even after a restart, and the teams keep following their players after halftime.

**Save players to teams:** new in Live Control. Once the teams on screen are right (swap first if they aren't), press it. The in-game players on CT are saved to the team shown on CT, and the players on T to the team shown on T, by their SteamID64:
- players MatchFrame doesn't know yet are added with their in-game name;
- players already saved move to that team;
- coaches and bots are left out.

From then on MatchFrame recognises the teams by their players on every map, with no swapping needed.

## Demo Library

**Fixed:** demos failing with "hash failed: … QUEUED → HASHED is not allowed".

**Why:** a large demo takes more than 5 seconds to check, and the next check started on the same demo before the first had finished. The second one then marked the demo as failed, even though the first had already queued it.

**Now:**
- checks never overlap;
- a demo is only ever checked once at a time;
- demos this already hit are queued again by themselves when the program starts. You don't need to do anything.
