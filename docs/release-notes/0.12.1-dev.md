# MatchFrame Esports 0.12.1-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.12.0:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Default player picture

**Settings → General → Default player picture:** upload one picture (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB). From then on, it is shown for every player without a picture of their own:
- the Teams and Players lists, and the team editor's player tiles;
- MatchFrame HUDs;
- imported HUDs (hud.json format);
- the Team Comms speaker strip.

**Which picture is shown:**
- A player's own picture always comes first.
- In MatchFrame HUDs, the side pictures under **HUD Library → HUD images** come next, when you've set them. The default player picture is used where they aren't set.
- On the speaker strip, a placeholder set in Team Comms comes before the default player picture.

**Changing it:** **Replace** or **Remove** it at any time. HUDs update straight away.

The player editor's **Avatar** field links to the setting.
