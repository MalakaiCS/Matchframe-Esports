# MatchFrame Esports 0.12.0-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.11.0:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. The installer has a new file name, so this one update downloads in full; later updates are small again.

## Now called MatchFrame Esports

The program, its windows, menus, messages, HUDs, the website and the installer (`MatchFrameEsports-Setup-0.12.0.exe`) all say MatchFrame Esports.

**Nothing of yours moves or breaks:**
- Your teams, players, matches, pictures, HUDs and settings are all there after the update.
- The program stays where it is installed, and keeps updating itself.
- **HUDs you imported** (hud.json format) carry on working and are listed under **Imported**. The OBS browser sources you already made for them keep working as they are.
- **OBS:** the scenes, sources and transitions MatchFrame made before (spelled "Matchframe …") are found and used again; nothing is made twice.
- **Folders** keep their place, for example your data folder and Videos\Matchframe Highlights.

## Teams, Players and Matches

All three work the same way: a list, a full-page form to create or edit, then **Save and Exit** back to the updated list. **Cancel** leaves everything as it was, and MatchFrame asks before throwing away changes you haven't saved, including when you close the window.

**Lists:**
- Search, filters (country, team including Unassigned, archived), sorting by column.
- Tick several rows to delete them together.
- Your search, filters and place in the list are kept when you come back.

**Teams:** name, short name, country with its flag, and a logo (PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 2 MB; a square picture of 200 × 200 or more works best).
- **Assigned Players** shows the team's players. Click one to edit them.
- **Assign Existing Player** and **Add Player** (the new player is put on the team).

**Players:** nickname, first and last name, country, SteamID64, team and picture.
- The nickname is what the broadcast shows. The SteamID64 links the player to the game, and changing the nickname keeps that link.
- A player without a SteamID64 can be saved and is marked "won't match live CS2 data".
- The same SteamID64 can't be used twice.
- A team can have any number of players, substitutes included. Changing a player's team updates both teams straight away.

**Matches:** **Ended**, **Current** and **Future** tabs.
- **Make Active** is its own button, and only one match is active for the HUD at a time. A match's start time never makes it active by itself.
- **The match screen:**
  - Left: both teams with their logos, the series score (maps won), best of 1, 2, 3 or 5, date and time, tournament.
  - Right: the map veto in order. Each entry has Pick, Ban or Decider, the team, the map from your map pool, the other team's starting side (CT, T or not chosen) and **Reversed sides**.
  - You can save before the veto is finished, to set up a match ahead of time.
- A finished map is counted once in the series score, and a live result never overwrites what you are editing.
- Captain veto links, map results, stat corrections and the match's thumbnails and stingers are in sections lower on the same screen.

**Deleting:** MatchFrame names what you are deleting and what is linked to it. Something still in use (a team in a match or with players, the active match) isn't deleted; you're offered **Archive** instead.

**Import from Excel:**
- **Import Teams** and **Import Players** take an .xlsx file. Download the template from the import window.
- A preview shows every row's problems before anything is added.
- A team name that matches no team, or more than one, is an error, never a guess. So is a SteamID64 that is already used.
