# Matchframe Esports 0.10.2-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen in CS2 or OBS on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.10.1:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Automatic replays play again

The main PC said "The program isn't on the live scene" while OBS showed your live scene, so the replay PC's clips waited for **Approve** and **Play** instead of playing by themselves.

**Why:** Matchframe's program could stay in another mode while OBS was already back on the live scene:
- After a replay you played by hand, it stayed in "replay playing".
- After OBS reconnected, it stayed in "degraded".

Automatic replays only play from live, so they waited.

**Now:**
- **Follows OBS:** whenever OBS shows your live scene, the program counts as live again, whether you switched there by hand or OBS reconnected on it.
- **Clips played by hand:** after **Approve** and **Play**, the program goes back to the live scene when the clip ends, like an automatic replay.
- **Plainer status:** if the program really isn't live, the status says which mode it's in, and that **Return live** at the top of **Replays & Production** puts it back.

**Until you update:** press **Return live** once on the main PC.

## Support file: replays

The support file (**Settings → Diagnostics**) now includes:
- why automatic replays play or not;
- the program mode;
- the replay scene and media source;
- the link to the replay PC and its NDI recording;
- the last 40 clips with their state.
