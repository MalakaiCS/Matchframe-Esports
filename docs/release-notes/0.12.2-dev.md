## The active match's logos in game

**Fixed:** the active match's team names and logos didn't show in the HUDs once the game was live.

**Why:** MatchFrame only put a saved team on a side when at least 3 of that side's players had their SteamID64 saved and were on that team. Without them, the HUD fell back to CS2's own team name and showed no logo.

**Now:** the active match's two teams are always on screen, in every HUD (MatchFrame and imported). MatchFrame decides which team is on CT and which on T, in this order:
1. **SteamID64s:** the players' saved SteamID64s, as before. One side recognised is enough; the other side is the other team.
2. **CS2's team names:** if you set them in CS2 (mp_teamname), and they match a team's name or short name.
3. **A few players:** even one or two players with saved SteamID64s, if they agree.
4. **The veto:** the starting side from the match's veto.
5. **A guess:** Team 1 on CT.

From then on, each team stays with its players after halftime.

**In Live Control:** a line under the match header shows **On screen: CT … · T …** and how MatchFrame decided. If it was taken from the veto or guessed, a **Swap teams** button flips them.

**Best result:** save each player's SteamID64 (Players → Edit). Then MatchFrame recognises the teams by itself, and their names and pictures show too.
