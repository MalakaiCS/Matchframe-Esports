# Matchframe Esports 0.10.6-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.10.5:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Vector and Lineup update to 1.2.0 and the Pro CS2 HUD to 1.3.0 (they gain the replay graphic); your settings for them stay.

Everything below is under **Replays & Production**.

## Replay graphic

While a replay is on air, a **REPLAY** tag with the round comes in, then a lower third showing:
- the team and its logo, and the player;
- the weapon, headshot and who they killed;
- the size of the play: 2K, 3K, 4K, ACE, or a 1vN clutch.

**How it looks:**
- **Each HUD in its own style:** Vector, Lineup, the Pro CS2 HUD and the built-in Broadcast HUD. Imported HUDs (kaiHUD and others) use the Broadcast HUD's style.
- **Each clip:** the lower third changes for each clip of a break, and both go out after the last.
- **Side colours:** a play is shown in the side colour the player had in that round, so a first-half play keeps its colour after the swap.

**In OBS:**
- Your HUD in the live scene doesn't show it.
- **Replay graphic → Add replay graphic to the OBS replay scene** puts a "Matchframe Replay Graphic" browser source on top of your replay scene. Its "B" copy gets it too, so it stays steady through the fades.
- In the same panel you can turn it off or pick which HUD's style it uses.

## Controls while replays play

- **Skip:** the next clip now.
- **Hold / Resume:** pauses the clip on air. 1:50 still cuts back to live.
- **Stop and go live**
- **In the upcoming list:** **Exclude** a clip, or **Pin first**.
- **Hotkeys:**
  - **In the program:** Ctrl+Shift+F5, F6 and F7 while it's in front.
  - **Global hotkeys** work while CS2 or OBS is in front. They're off until you set them.
- **Stream Deck:** through Bitfocus Companion's HTTP requests or a Stream Deck web-request plugin. docs/integrations/companion-streamdeck.md has the recipes.

## Sound on replays

- **The replay feed:** it now records the replay PC's sound with its picture, so clips cut from it have game sound. Clips the replay PC sends from its own OBS replay buffer carry whatever that OBS records. Tick the game's sound into Audio Track 1 there.
- **Replay volume:** a setting for the replay clips.
- **Live game audio during replays:**
  - Pick the OBS audio sources that are the live game, and choose how far down they go (mute, −10, −20 or −30 dB).
  - They come down as the replays start and go back to exactly where they were afterwards, however the replays end. That includes a restart in the middle.
  - Casters' mics are never touched unless you pick them.
  - vMix works the same way.

## Test replays

**Test replays** plays two sample clips through the whole chain:
- your Replay in transition;
- the fade, the graphic and the sound;
- your Replay out transition, back to live.

It then lists each step as OK or what went wrong, and how to fix it. It asks first, since it plays on your program output, and won't run while a round is live.

## Switching scenes yourself (multiview)

**What changed:** before, if you put one of your own scenes on air in OBS (with a multiview plugin, say), Matchframe still thought the program was live. Automatic replays, and the break and studio scenes, could then cut your scene away.

**Now:**
- While OBS shows a scene of yours, Matchframe changes nothing until OBS is back on your live scene: no replays, no automatic break or studio scenes, and no cut back at 1:50.
- The status says it's waiting for you; it isn't an error.
- **During the replays:** if you switch to a scene of yours, Matchframe stops its replays, puts the game audio back, and waits.
- **Back on live:** if the break still has time, its replays play.

## Highlight reel

**Highlight reel** makes one MP4 of a map's played clips, or its best N:
- **Order and fades:** in round order, with fades.
- **Graphic:** optionally with the replay graphic burned in.
- **Format:** 1080p at up to 60 fps, with sound, ready for social media.

**Keeping the clips:** the clips are kept for the current and previous map, up to 3 GB (you can change this), even after the replay list drops them.

**ffmpeg:** if it's missing, the panel offers to download it.

## Storage on another drive

**Storage** chooses a folder, on any drive, for:
- **Replay clips and recording:** the replay PC's clips and the replay feed's recording. These write the most while a match is on.
- **Highlights:** the clips kept for reels and the reels themselves.

**Choose folder…** opens a folder picker. The panel shows the free space on that drive.

New files go to the new folder from then on. Clips already saved stay where they are and still work.

## Also fixed

- **A clip that played late:** a clip waiting in the replay scene's "B" copy could keep playing while hidden, when OBS ignored the pause. It then came on air near its end.
- **Feed clips cut slightly early:** clips cut from the replay feed were cut about 30–60 ms early.
