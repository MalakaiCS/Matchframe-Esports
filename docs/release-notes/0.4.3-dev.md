# Matchframe Esports 0.4.3-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Installed and checked on a Windows CI runner and against a stand-in CS2 (a test console and test game data). Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.4.2:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Everything in [0.4.2](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/releases/tag/v0.4.2) is included.

## Cameras for every named area

0.4.2 had cameras for the bombsites, mid and the spawns. Now the rest of the map's named areas get cameras too: Banana, Long, Connector, Heaven, Ramp, Apartments and so on.

- **391 cameras** instead of 104, covering 200 of the 227 named areas on the nine active-duty maps. The 81 camera paths are unchanged.

  | Map | Cameras | Areas with a camera |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ancient | 31 | 15 of 19 |
  | Anubis | 57 | 28 of 28 |
  | Dust II | 54 | 26 of 26 |
  | Inferno | 46 | 24 of 26 |
  | Mirage | 46 | 23 of 26 |
  | Nuke | 42 | 24 of 29 |
  | Overpass | 41 | 21 of 26 |
  | Train | 36 | 19 of 24 |
  | Vertigo | 38 | 20 of 23 |

- **The same checks as 0.4.2:** each new camera is placed on CS2's walkable floor and checked against CS2's collision geometry. It has clear space around it, sees the area's centre and at least 45 % of the area (86 % on a typical camera), and most of its view is open.
- **Where they're used:** the Auto Director uses them for big fights (Observer & Cinematics) and nade cams (Full Action Observer) anywhere on the map, not only on the sites. Freeze time still uses the bombsite, mid and spawn cameras and paths.
- **No camera yet:** 27 small or enclosed areas, where no spot passes the checks. These are mostly tunnels, ladders and small rooms, for example Nuke Vents and Secret, Overpass Pit, Train Ladder and Mirage Shop.

As before, nobody has looked through these cameras in CS2 yet. If one looks wrong, mark it **Invalidated** in **Maps & Cameras** and it's never used again.

## Chase cam

The Auto Director now shows some moments from behind the player (CS2's third-person chase view) instead of through their eyes:

- while they plant the bomb;
- while they defuse it;
- when their team wins the round and they're still alive.

It goes back to first person when the moment is over. Gunfights stay in first person, where the crosshair matters.

- **Switch it off:** untick **Chase cam** on the Auto Director card.
- **By hand:** hold **Shift** when you click a player card or press their number key in Live Control.
- **How it confirms itself:** the first time, Matchframe asks CS2 where the camera is (`getpos`). If the camera didn't move behind the player, it tries CS2's other `spec_mode` number once, and keeps whichever works.

## Checked automatically

- **Stand-in CS2:** the Auto Director shows a planting player in the chase view, `getpos` confirms it, and it goes back to first person once the bomb is planted. A Shift-click picks the chase view by hand. The console tests also cover learning the other chase number, and going back to the original command when neither number works.
- **Geometry checks:** all 391 cameras pass the clearance and line-of-sight checks against the CS2 meshes.
- **Map packs:** all nine validate against the map-pack schema, and the built-in camera test still passes.
- **The installed program on a Windows runner:** the same checks as 0.4.2.
