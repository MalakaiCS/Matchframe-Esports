# Matchframe Esports 0.10.7-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.10.6:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Highlights on air

A new **Highlights on air** panel under **Replays & Production** plays a map's best clips on air when you press **Play highlights**, then goes back to the scene you came from.

**Setting up:**
- **Set up OBS highlights scene** makes a "Matchframe Highlights" scene with a video source, plus its "B" copy for fades. It also puts the replay graphic on top: the same browser source as in your replay scene.
- **Your own scene:** choose it and its media source instead, if you'd rather.

**What plays:**
- **What:** this map, the last half of this map, or the previous map.
- **How many:** the best 5 (default), 8, 10 or 15, or all played clips. You can also include clips that weren't played.
- **Preview:** the panel lists the clips with their round and length, and the total running time.

**Playing:**
- Clips play in round order, with fades between them and the replay graphic on each.
- The live game audio is turned down, as for replays.
- **Skip**, **Hold** and **Stop** work, and the status reads *Highlight 3 of 8: …*.
- **Start and stop from:** the button, the Stream Deck (`highlights_play`, `highlights_stop`), or hotkeys. In the program, Ctrl+Shift+F9 plays and Ctrl+Shift+F10 stops. Global hotkeys are off until you set them.

**Transitions:**
- **Highlights in** and **Highlights out** are under Production automation, stingers included.
- By default they're the same as Replay in and Replay out.

**Afterwards:**
- The program goes back to where it was: your own scene (your analysis scene, say), the live scene, or the studio or break scene.
- If that scene was removed meanwhile, it goes to the live scene.

**Switching yourself:** if you put another scene on in OBS during highlights, they stop and Matchframe changes nothing more.

**Rounds:**
- Highlights don't start while a round is live.
- If halftime ends while they play, they cut to the live scene by your **Back to live** time on the round clock (1:50 by default).
- Round replays never start while highlights are on air.
- Starting highlights in a break replaces that break's replays.

## Don't show highlights at halftime

A switch in the panel, **on by default**.

**On:**
- No highlights start at halftime: not automatically, and not from Play, the hotkeys or the Stream Deck.
- Play is greyed out with "Highlights at halftime are off".
- Halftime here also covers the seconds after a half's last round, so Play pressed as the half ends doesn't run through halftime.

**Off:** you can play highlights at halftime, and **Start by themselves at halftime** appears (off by default).

**Already playing:** highlights that started before halftime aren't cut short.

**Stream Deck:** `highlights_halftime` switches it (on, off or toggle), and the tally shows its state for a button.

## Also

- **When a map ends:** optionally, highlights can start by themselves. This is off by default and only happens from the live scene, never from your own scene.
- **vMix:** works too, with its own Highlights in and out. vMix goes back to the input that was on air before.
- **Sponsor bugs:** they're hidden during highlights, as during replays.
