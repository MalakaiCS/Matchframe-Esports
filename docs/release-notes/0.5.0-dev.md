# Matchframe Esports 0.5.0-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Installed and checked on a Windows CI runner, against a stand-in CS2, and with a real OBS on Linux. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.4.3:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Everything in [0.4.3](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/releases/tag/v0.4.3) is included.

## Replay PC and automatic replays

Use a second computer as a **replay PC**. It films the kills your main camera misses, and the main PC replays them between rounds by itself.

1. **On the main PC:**
   - Open **Replays & Production → Replay PC → This is the main PC**.
   - Tick **Let other computers on the network connect**.
   - Note the address, and press **Make a replay PC code**.
   - Press **Set up OBS replay scene**, and tick **Play replays by themselves**.
2. **On the replay PC:**
   - Start CS2 and spectate the same match.
   - Connect OBS with a Game Capture.
   - In **Replay PC → This is the replay PC**, type the main PC's address and the code.
   - Start the Auto Director.

What happens:

- The replay PC's Auto Director films whoever the main camera *isn't* showing: the other side of the same fight, or another fight.
- When a kill happens that the main camera missed (or showed only from the player who died), the replay PC saves the last seconds from OBS and sends the clip to the main PC.
- The main PC plays replays in the break after the round: from a few seconds after it ends until just before freeze time ends. They never play during a live round.
  - **Order:** a won clutch first, then 3K+, then missed kills.
  - **How many:** 2 per break by default.
  - **Speed:** real time, or slow motion (75 % or 50 %).
- **Without a replay PC**, the main PC can still replay big plays its own camera showed (a 3K or more, a won 1v2+) from its own OBS replay buffer.
- **Replay transition:** pick a Stinger for cuts to and from replays in **OBS scenes**.

Step by step: [Replay PC and automatic replays](https://github.com/MalakaiCS/Matchframe-Esports/blob/v0.5.0/docs/operations/replay-pc.md).

## Clutch mode

When one player is left alone against the other team, the Auto Director stays with them:

- no cuts to cameras;
- the chase view while no opponent is near, and first person again as one closes in;
- a **1v3** banner on Matchframe HUDs;
- a replay in the break if they win.

Untick **Clutch mode** on the Auto Director card to switch it off.

## Cutting before the fight

The program now has a free-space map of every active-duty map, made from CS2's own collision geometry. With it, the Auto Director knows when two opponents see each other, or are about to (a corner opening at their current speed). It cuts to the fight before the shooting starts. Players with walls between them no longer count as about to fight.

Checked against the exact geometry on 1,500 sightlines per map: it misses 5–13 % of clear sightlines, and saw through a wall at most once in about 1,350 blocked ones.

## Follow cams

With cinematic cameras on, a free camera now flies behind and a little above a player running somewhere with no fight close: a lurk, a rotation, the bomb carrier walking onto a site. It lasts about 4.5 s and cuts away as soon as a fight starts. Every frame is checked against the free-space map, so the camera stays clear of walls and comes in closer when a wall is behind the player. Untick **Follow cams** on the Auto Director card to switch it off.

## HUD graphics timed to the shots

While the Auto Director has the camera, Matchframe HUDs show:

- the economy panel in freeze time, then the round MVP (CS2's own MVP);
- a player's card (kills, assists, deaths) when the camera has stayed on them a while with nothing happening. Only on a player view, never over a camera, and gone when the view changes;
- the clutch banner.

Settings are in **Replays & Production → HUD graphics**.

## OBS scenes

In **Replays & Production → OBS scenes**, pick a scene for live, replay, break and studio:

- **Tactical timeouts and pauses** switch to the break scene, and back when play resumes.
- **Map end** switches to the studio scene, and back when the next map starts.

Matchframe only changes the scene from the live scene, and never undoes a scene you chose yourself.

## kaiHUD: Player Highlight, Match stats and the rest

kaiHUD's extra pages couldn't be opened at all before: Player Highlight (*player-stats*), Match stats (*match-stats*), *match-preview*, *veto* and *baseboard*. Now:

- **The pages:** they are listed under **HUD pages** in the HUD's details, each with **Copy URL** for OBS.
- **Team logos:** they now show in kaiHUD. They were broken images before.
- **Sponsor images:** the baseboard sponsor images can be uploaded in **Appearance** (**Add images**).
- **Shown by themselves:** in **HUD graphics → LHM HUD pages**, pick the kaiHUD pages' OBS sources. Matchframe shows Player Highlight for the round MVP in freeze time, and Match stats at half time and at the end of the map.

kaiHUD 1.0.2 was checked page by page in a browser against test data: Player Highlight, Match stats, match preview and baseboard showed the right players, stats, logos, team colours and sponsor images, with no errors.

## Checked automatically

- **Two programs** (main PC and replay PC) with a stand-in OBS:
  - linking with a code;
  - the replay PC avoiding the main player;
  - a missed kill sent with its label;
  - a kill the main camera showed not sent;
  - the replay after the round, then back to live;
  - a won 1v2 replayed first.
- **Against a real OBS** (Linux, Xvfb): the replay PC's buffer saved and sent, the one-click replay scene, the clip played after the round, back to the live scene.
- **Director:**
  - clutch mode (locks on, no cameras);
  - the clutch chase lead-up with its margin;
  - sightlines (a corner about to open vs walls);
  - follow cam choice;
  - avoiding the main player.
- **Follow camera** against a test grid: behind and above, keeps up, closer at a wall, ends when the player dies.
- **Console streaming** of the follow cam.
- **Graphics timing, OBS automation**, and the new screens.
- **The installed program on a Windows runner:** the same checks as 0.4.3.
