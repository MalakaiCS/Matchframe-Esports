# Matchframe Esports 0.10.5-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner. Not yet seen on your PC.**

**Coming from 0.10.4:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded.

## Replays play on until 1:50

Replays no longer have to finish before the round starts. They keep playing into the next round and cut back to live by the time the round clock reads 1:50.
- The time comes from the game's own round clock. With CS2's 1:55 rounds, that is about 5 s into the round.
- A bomb plant or the round ending stops the replays at once.
- **Setting:** under **Automatic replays**, **Back to live** offers 1:52, 1:50, 1:45, 1:40, 1:35 or 1:30 on the clock, or **Before the round starts** (the old way).
- The extra time fits about one more clip per break.

## Clips fade into each other

OBS can only fade between two scenes, not between two clips in one media source. So Matchframe now keeps a copy of your replay scene:
- The copy is named after your scene with **B** at the end.
- It holds the same overlays in the same place, and its own media source with the same position, size, crop and filters.
- Clips take turns on the two scenes, and OBS fades from one to the next. There's no black frame, and your overlays stay steady through the fade.

**Setting:** **Between replays** is a fade of 0.3, 0.5 (default), 0.75 or 1 s, or **Cut**.

**What Matchframe changes in OBS:**
- It makes the copy when you press **Set up OBS replay scene**, and updates it when your replay scene changes.
- On your replay media source it turns off three options: **Restart playback when source becomes active**, **Close file when inactive** and **Show nothing when playback ends**. With them on, every second fade would show the start of the clip.

**When there can't be a copy:** if your media source is inside a group, or isn't a Media Source, replays play one after another in one source as in 0.10.4. The panel says why.

**vMix** fades from one replay to the next too.

## Pick your transitions, stingers included

Under **Replays & Production**:
- **Replay in:** from live to the first replay.
- **Replay out:** from the last replay back to live. The default is the same as Replay in.
- **Between replays:** a fade or a cut (see above).
- **Other scenes:** to and from your break and studio scenes, whether the automation or you switch them in Matchframe.

**Choosing a stinger:** each list shows every transition in your OBS. A Stinger has to be made once in OBS (**Scene Transitions → + → Stinger**, then choose its video). After that you can pick it here.

**Afterwards:** Matchframe puts your own OBS transition back when its transition has finished, including a long stinger.

**vMix** has its own Replay in and Replay out.

## Replays from an earlier round

**The problem:** a clip from an earlier round could play in the next round's replays, and first.

**Why:** CS2 moves its round counter on as soon as a round is won. So a kill after that (an exit frag, or a saving player hunted down) was saved under the next round.

**Now:**
- Kills, clips and bookmarks made after a round ends count for the round that ended.
- Once a round's replays are over, no unplayed clip from that round or an earlier one ever plays automatically.
- A clip with no round never plays automatically. Neither does a clip from before its round started, for example after a round restart or backup.
- The list under **Automatic replays** says why such a clip won't play.

## OBS Studio Mode

Replays work with Studio Mode on. This was checked in OBS 30 with Duplicate Scene, Swap Scenes and Duplicate Sources on.
- Your preview is left as you set it. If OBS's **Swap Scenes** changes it, Matchframe puts it back.
- Pressing **Transition** during the replays counts as you taking the program, and the replays stop.

## Also fixed

**Return to live recorded twice:** the program's history no longer records the return to live twice when OBS follows the program back.
