# Matchframe Esports 0.7.0-dev — development build

**Unsigned development build. Checked automatically on Linux and on a Windows CI runner, and on seven professional demos replayed through the director. Not yet used against a real CS2 client.**

**Coming from 0.6.8:** the program updates itself. Press **Restart now** in the status bar when the update has downloaded. Nothing to change. The new options are on the Auto Director card and in Training.

## The Auto Director learns from demos

- **The demo-trained POV model is built in and on.** It was trained on seven pro matches (Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage, Nuke). For every living player it estimates the chance their POV shows a kill in the next 2 seconds, and half of the director's "a fight is coming" score now comes from it.
  - On those seven matches, each judged by a model that never saw it, the director showed **74 % of kills instead of 71 %**.
  - **102 of 141 opening kills** were shown, against 96.
  - It cut **about 10 % less often**.
  - **Training → What the demos teach** turns it off, or switches to a model you train on your own demos (the task "POV shows a kill within 2 s").
- **Multi-frag awareness:** after a kill, with enemies close, the director goes to (or stays on) that player, since another kill often follows. It only leaves a POV that is quiet; leaving one that was about to fight cost more kills than it brought. With the model, **53 % of multi-frags** are now shown from the killer's eyes, against 45 %.
- **Director test bench** (Training): your read demos replayed through the director as if live, with CS2's switch delay. It shows how many kills, opening kills and multi-frags it showed, how early, and how often it cut. It also shows the same without the demo model and without multi-frag awareness, so you can see what each adds. It never runs during a live broadcast.

## Cinematics more like a broadcast

- **Different freeze times:**
  - every third round, one longer shot of one team;
  - a team on an eco gets no team shot;
  - team shots from high up, low or from the side as well as at eye height;
  - cameras used in the last rounds give way to others.
- **Big moments:**
  - a wide opener with the map's camera before each pistol round;
  - the team on match point first, and longer;
  - slow shots of the map and the teams at half time and at the end of the map (the winners first).
- **The winners after the round:** 2 seconds after the round ends (the last kill stays on air first), a shot of the team that won, where they stand. Every round by default; the card can set big rounds only, or never.
- **Camera paths through HLAE (experimental, off):** in the Observer tools + HLAE launch mode, camera paths can be played by HLAE's `mirv_campath`, which moves the camera every frame. If HLAE isn't there, Matchframe moves the camera itself as before.

## Your own cameras

- **Camera walkthrough** (Maps): every camera of the map being played, a few seconds each, with **Keep** and **Hide**. Hidden cameras are never used. The checks Matchframe runs can't see trees, wires, flags or lighting; you can.
- **Save the CS2 view as a camera:** put the free camera where you like it in CS2 and press **Ctrl+Shift+F11**. That view becomes one of your cameras for this map, used for freeze time and fights. There's also a button on the Maps screen and an entry in the Automation menu.
